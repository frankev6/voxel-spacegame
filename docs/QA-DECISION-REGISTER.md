## How to audit authorship and authority

Treat Tech lead decisions and implementation defaults as agent-selected unless an exact owner instruction establishes otherwise. Preserve explicit owner decisions as such. Some early entries belong to predecessor workers; the coordinator cannot honestly claim personal authorship of every historical row. Inspect linked implementation commits and ADR provenance for attribution. A bare `Accepted` ADR status is not evidence of human sign-off.

The complete ledger question register is reproduced below without filtering: this avoids omitting choices hidden in inconsistent table schemas, defaults or unresolved owner rows. Some old rows contain stale acceptance notes; current implementation, task log and evidence control what was actually delivered. Blank answers are not approvals. Also read each spec's §10 and every ADR below; not all local decisions have a perfectly reconciled central mirror. Record unlogged deviations as findings rather than assuming authorization. This is the complete recorded trail available in these sources, not a guarantee that every code-level judgment was documented.
