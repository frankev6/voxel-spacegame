Ship lines: silhouettes first

Round 4, step 1. Plan views from above, bow at the top, drawn as solid silhouettes. Pale shapes are a second tone, yellow or gilt rings are turrets, and orange marks the engines (and CRUCIBLE's open melt). Every card in a row is at the same scale.
