# Mood board — DRYDOCK

*Industrial used-future. Bone and graphite plating, safety-coded systems, stencils and navigation lights; black space; the alien is clean and wrong.* — [DESIGN-SYSTEM §5](../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#5-direction-a--drydock)

**What to look for:** hard, single-source light on light-valued hulls against near-black space; systems you can name by their colour coding before you read a label; destruction that looks like real metal failing (scorch, char, glowing cut edges, girders behind the plating); an alien that is unsettling because it is too clean.

**Images:** links only for now. Public-domain NASA photographs are the planned image set (the ISS truss, Apollo 15's service module, the McCandless spacewalk, *Earthrise*, a post-flight heat shield); they are added once the owner approves downloading them.

## Ships and stations

### Nostromo and the Alien hardware
- **Creator:** Ron Cobb (concept design); Ridley Scott (director)
- **Year:** 1979
- **Take:** Layered utilitarian forms, worn paint and stencilled codes that make a ship read as a working machine rather than a hero prop.
- **Touchstone:** Ron Cobb
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Cobb

### ISS Integrated Truss Structure
- **Creator:** NASA and partner agencies (photographs: NASA crews)
- **Year:** 2000–2009
- **Take:** Scale shown through repetition of one structural module, which is exactly how a 1M-cell station should read at 300 m.
- **Touchstone:** NASA and ESA hardware photography
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_Truss_Structure

### Apollo service module in orbit (Apollo 15 photographs)
- **Creator:** NASA
- **Year:** 1971
- **Take:** Big flat panels broken by a few dense functional areas (the open instrument bay, thruster quads), our ratio of plain hull to functional blocks.
- **Touchstone:** NASA and ESA hardware photography
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_command_and_service_module

### The Expanse: Rocinante and Tycho Station
- **Creator:** Seth Reed (production designer); Alcon Television
- **Year:** 2015–2022
- **Take:** Practical ships whose function is legible from the outside and stations whose size comes from lights, windows and traffic.
- **Touchstone:** The Expanse
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expanse_(TV_series)

## Space and lighting

### First untethered spacewalk (Bruce McCandless II, STS-41-B)
- **Creator:** NASA (photograph by the Challenger crew)
- **Year:** 1984
- **Take:** One hard sun, no fill, a pure black sky and a white figure: the value hierarchy of P1 in a single photograph.
- **Touchstone:** NASA and ESA hardware photography
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_McCandless_II

### Earthrise
- **Creator:** William Anders, NASA (Apollo 8)
- **Year:** 1968
- **Take:** A hard planetary terminator and a thin atmosphere rim against black, which is our planet rule (§5.4).
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthrise

### Homeworld: Deserts of Kharak
- **Creator:** Blackbird Interactive
- **Year:** 2016
- **Take:** Weathered industrial palettes under a single hard key light that still reads at strategy-camera distances.
- **Touchstone:** Homeworld: Deserts of Kharak
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeworld:_Deserts_of_Kharak

## Destruction and VFX

### Hardspace: Shipbreaker
- **Creator:** Blackbird Interactive
- **Year:** 2022
- **Take:** Ships that stay readable while being cut apart, with safety colours marking what is dangerous to cut.
- **Touchstone:** Hardspace: Shipbreaker
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardspace:_Shipbreaker

### Ship breaking yards (Alang, Chittagong)
- **Creator:** Real-world industry (documentary photography)
- **Year:** 1983–present
- **Take:** Hulls opened in section show frames, girders and rust behind the plating, our exposed-internals layers.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_breaking

### Heat shields after atmospheric entry
- **Creator:** NASA (post-flight photographs of Apollo command modules)
- **Year:** 1968–1972
- **Take:** The gradient from clean paint to scorch to char on one surface, which is the damage ladder of stages 1–3.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry

### Gravity: the destruction of the station
- **Creator:** Alfonso Cuarón (director); Framestore (visual effects)
- **Year:** 2013
- **Take:** Silent breakup where chunks keep their momentum and tumble, so nothing pops out of existence.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_(2013_film)

## UI and typography

### ISO 3864 safety colours and signs
- **Creator:** International Organization for Standardization
- **Year:** 1984–2011
- **Take:** A colour-plus-shape code that people read without training, the model for our function colours and glyphs.
- **Touchstone:** ISO 3864
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3864

### Alien: Isolation interfaces
- **Creator:** Creative Assembly
- **Year:** 2014
- **Take:** Low-fidelity, utilitarian screens with plain rectangles and terse codes, the voice of the panel headers (`INV-01`).
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien:_Isolation

### NASA graphics standards ("the worm")
- **Creator:** Richard Danne and Bruce Blackburn
- **Year:** 1975
- **Take:** A disciplined, engineered type system applied to hardware and documents alike.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_insignia

### Barlow and IBM Plex Mono (the proposed type)
- **Creator:** Jeremy Tribby (Barlow); Mike Abbink and Bold Monday (IBM Plex)
- **Year:** 2017
- **Take:** Condensed grotesque headings and a sober mono for numbers, both under the SIL Open Font License.
- **Link:** https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow

## Otherness

### The monolith in 2001: A Space Odyssey
- **Creator:** Stanley Kubrick (director); Arthur C. Clarke (co-writer)
- **Year:** 1968
- **Take:** A perfect, featureless object inside a gritty human world is more frightening than any monster, the template for the Vanguard.
- **Touchstone:** 2001: A Space Odyssey
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monolith_(Space_Odyssey)

### Under the Skin: the black void
- **Creator:** Jonathan Glazer (director)
- **Year:** 2013
- **Take:** A matte black that swallows light and figures, the feel of the Silence erasing what it touches.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Skin_(2013_film)

### Rendezvous with Rama
- **Creator:** Arthur C. Clarke
- **Year:** 1973
- **Take:** An alien artefact explored, never explained, whose geometry follows rules humans do not use.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendezvous_with_Rama

## Anti-references

### The prototype's flight view (`05-flying`)
- **Creator:** The old `spaceship-game` prototype
- **Year:** 2025–2026
- **Take:** Avoid a bright salmon nebula that outshines the ships, which breaks rule L1 and pillar 1.
- **Link:** [05-flying.png](../../reference/audit-2026-10/screenshots/05-flying.png)

### Star Trek (2009): lens flares and gloss
- **Creator:** J. J. Abrams (director)
- **Year:** 2009
- **Take:** Avoid anamorphic flares and glossy white surfaces that bloom, since DRYDOCK light is hard and plain.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(2009_film)

### Generic grey block kits (Space Engineers default look)
- **Creator:** Keen Software House
- **Year:** 2019
- **Take:** Avoid uniform mid-grey plating with blue trim that makes every faction and every block look alike.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Engineers
