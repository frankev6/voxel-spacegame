# Mood board — NOCTURNE

*Dark glossy hulls traced by thin light-lines, glyphs of light on every system, deep blue-black space with luminous filaments. Damage is darkness spreading.* — [DESIGN-SYSTEM §6](../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md#6-direction-b--nocturne)

**What to look for:** dark forms that you see because of their edges, reflections and a few thin lines of light; skies with structure (filaments) but no bright cores behind the action; light as the carrier of both identity and damage (light dying, white-hot cracks); restraint, so a 30-ship fight does not turn into bloom soup.

**Images:** links only for now. NASA-only public-domain astronomy images (Hubble and Webb releases credited to NASA alone) are the planned image set; ESA-credited versions are CC BY 4.0 and stay links. They are added once the owner approves downloading them.

## Ships and stations

### Arrival: the shell
- **Creator:** Denis Villeneuve (director); Patrice Vermette (production designer)
- **Year:** 2016
- **Take:** A dark, seamless monolithic hull with no visible detail reads as more advanced and more threatening than greebles do.
- **Touchstone:** Arrival
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrival_(film)

### EVE Online ship designs
- **Creator:** CCP Games
- **Year:** 2003–present
- **Take:** Dark metal hulls given identity by thin lit seams and a few faction-coloured lights against a bright nebula.
- **Touchstone:** EVE Online
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_Online

### Event Horizon: the ship
- **Creator:** Paul W. S. Anderson (director); Joseph Bennett (production designer)
- **Year:** 1997
- **Take:** A near-black silhouette lit only by a few window strips, which shows how far darkness can go before a ship disappears.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event_Horizon_(film)

## Space and lighting

### Homeworld 2 skies
- **Creator:** Relic Entertainment
- **Year:** 2003
- **Take:** Painted, luminous nebula backdrops that silhouette dark fleets from behind, our rim-light idea at full scale.
- **Touchstone:** Homeworld 2
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeworld_2

### Blade Runner 2049: single-colour light fields
- **Creator:** Denis Villeneuve (director); Roger Deakins (cinematographer)
- **Year:** 2017
- **Take:** Whole scenes keyed to one coloured light with silhouettes against haze, the discipline behind one rim colour per direction.
- **Touchstone:** Blade Runner 2049
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner_2049

### Pillars of Creation
- **Creator:** NASA, Jeff Hester and Paul Scowen (Hubble Space Telescope)
- **Year:** 1995
- **Take:** Filament structure with dark dust lanes against glowing gas, the shape language of the NOCTURNE sky.
- **Touchstone:** Hubble
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation

### Cosmic Cliffs in NGC 3324
- **Creator:** NASA, ESA, CSA and STScI (James Webb Space Telescope)
- **Year:** 2022
- **Take:** A luminous ridge with fine filaments, which shows where the bright "filament cores" may sit (never behind the screen centre).
- **Touchstone:** JWST
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NGC_3324

## Destruction and VFX

### Tron: Legacy: derezzing
- **Creator:** Joseph Kosinski (director); Digital Domain (visual effects)
- **Year:** 2010
- **Take:** Objects breaking into dark cubes with glowing edges, almost exactly our cell debris, used sparingly.
- **Touchstone:** Tron: Legacy
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tron:_Legacy

### Pāhoehoe lava crust
- **Creator:** Natural phenomenon (USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory photography)
- **Year:** 1912–present
- **Take:** A black crust split by bright cracks that fade as they cool, the look of damage stages 2–3 and the hulk.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava

### Oblivion: drones and the Tet
- **Creator:** Joseph Kosinski (director)
- **Year:** 2013
- **Take:** Glossy machines with a single glowing eye that goes dark when destroyed, the power-down of a hulk.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblivion_(2013_film)

## UI and typography

### Dead Space: diegetic holographic UI
- **Creator:** EA Redwood Shores
- **Year:** 2008
- **Take:** Health and stasis shown on the character and menus projected into the world, the source of the arc HUD around the ship.
- **Touchstone:** Dead Space
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Space_(2008_video_game)

### Oblivion: interface graphics
- **Creator:** GMUNK (Bradley G. Munkowitz) and the film's screen-graphics team
- **Year:** 2013
- **Take:** Thin lines, wide uppercase labels and generous negative space on near-black, the NOCTURNE panel look.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblivion_(2013_film)

### Michroma and Inter (the proposed type)
- **Creator:** Vernon Adams (Michroma); Rasmus Andersson (Inter)
- **Year:** 2011–2017
- **Take:** A wide display face for headers only, and a neutral, very legible body face, both under the SIL Open Font License.
- **Link:** https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Michroma

## Otherness

### Annihilation: the Shimmer
- **Creator:** Alex Garland (director)
- **Year:** 2018
- **Take:** Otherness carried by light itself (prismatic, refracting, beautiful and wrong), the Vanguard's prism seams.
- **Touchstone:** Annihilation
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annihilation_(film)

### Under the Skin: the black void
- **Creator:** Jonathan Glazer (director)
- **Year:** 2013
- **Take:** A pure black that has no specular and no edge, the Silence as a hole cut out of the sky.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Skin_(2013_film)

## Anti-references

### Synthwave neon
- **Creator:** Music and visual genre (retro 1980s pastiche)
- **Year:** 2006–present
- **Take:** Avoid magenta-and-cyan neon on every surface, since NOCTURNE light is rare and therefore meaningful.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthwave

### Geometry Wars: bloom soup
- **Creator:** Bizarre Creations
- **Year:** 2005
- **Take:** Avoid full-screen bloom that is thrilling in a 2D arena but smears a 30-ship voxel fight into haze.
- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_Wars

### The prototype's identical white beams (`18-12-enemies`)
- **Creator:** The old `spaceship-game` prototype
- **Year:** 2025–2026
- **Take:** Avoid every weapon being the same white line, since light colour must carry damage type.
- **Link:** [18-12-enemies.png](../../reference/audit-2026-10/screenshots/18-12-enemies.png)
