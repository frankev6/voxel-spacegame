# Places for the extraction co-op: the heist station and the den

Status: design in progress, 2D low-res sketches only. Nothing here is built. Sketches are drawn by
`ArtSource/Places/sketch2d.py` (options in `sketches.py`, the Lantern in depth in `lantern.py`); one cell is one 8 m block.

## What the owner has decided

| Date | Decision | His words |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | The heist's prize is a station core | "the heist will require us stealing a station core, which are very hard to come by but will power up our den and allow us to start upgrading our own station, so this is a high stakes mission" |
| 2026-11-10 | The core is tethered; taking it trips the alarm; the crew can stay unseen | "the station core is tethered to the station and as soon as we take it, alarms go off, theres enemies all around, but dont see that we're quiet (unless we fck up)" |
| 2026-11-10 | The station is a busy place with many ways to do the job | "in my head its supposed to be a bustling station (like the hollow in how busy its is and crowded, and commerce) ... its more like a hitman map with multitude of options and ideas of how you could go about it" |
| 2026-11-10 | The den starts as a natural asteroid the crew digs, builds and defends | "I like the idea of starting out with a natural looking asteroid and building around it, the player should be able to dig themselves and place buildings/blocks in their den like how they edit the ship they can edit the den, buidling defenses as well, because some scenarios where the den gets attacked and you must defend it." |
| 2026-11-10 | Sketch in 2D low-res before any 3D | "just give me 2d low res options for the dens, and the heist station because you spend 30 minutes completely missaligned otherwise" |
| 2026-11-10 | Tollhouse Nine is the right kind of heist station; take it further | "Yes, deepen it" |

Rejected along the way: bespoke 3D block-outs (`ArtSource/DenHeist/`, never committed), the Hulk, Geode and Ring dens, the
Reliquary and Counting-House heists, the Rift as a flat wreck ("a straight 2d planar movement and the cargo is exposed
outside"), and round 1 of the 2D sketches (Stack, Wheel, Quarry: "just a box with a big shiny ball in it").
