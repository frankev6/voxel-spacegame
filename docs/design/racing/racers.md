# Racer ships: silhouette options (RACE-003)

**Decision (owner, 2026-10-17): "for ships i actually like all options you made, lets create all 6".** Later the same day: "remove the salvage tug ship, it has no place in this." Five are built: three Darts (Needle, Kite, Gimbal) and two Rams (Battering Ram, Hammerhead).

Two racers, three silhouette options each, for owner approval before any voxel build (SPEC-92 decision 2). The sheet is generated by `ArtSource/Racing/racer_silhouettes.py`.

![Racer silhouettes](img/racer_silhouettes.png)

| | Dart (Vestari) | Ram (Khuchad Clan) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Light, fast, avoids contact | Heavy, slow, makes contact |
| Hit force | Every hit throws it across the track | Barely moves; its impact driver shoves rivals and rocks |
| Ability | SLIP: a sideways dash, 6 s cooldown | SHOVE: a 60 m radial repulsor pulse, no damage, 10 s cooldown |
| Counter | The Ram, repulsor mines, engine wash | A clean Dart, EMP mines, tight hairpins |

Options:

| | Dart | Ram |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Needle.** A spear courier on one accelerator spine, with engine pods on swept pylons | **Battering ram.** A spiked wedge prow with the driver barrel through it, plus a salvaged pod bolted to port |
| 2 | **Kite.** A flying wing with a heat sail of radiator fins, so it can boost longer | **Salvage tug.** A grapple crane that tethers to pylons, wrecks and rivals, and a bumper bow |
| 3 | **Gimbal.** A cockpit pod inside a ring of eight thrusters; it corners best and is a circle silhouette | **Hammerhead.** A dock-bumper hammer with an impact driver at each tip, to spin rivals |

Picks before the decision (all six were chosen):
- **Needle against Battering Ram:** the clearest contrast in shape and in role.
- **Gimbal and the Salvage tug** are the most novel, because each brings a mechanic: omni-thrust, and the tether.

The ratings are design intent. RACE-008 builds the approved shapes from DESIGN-GUIDE blocks and measures them.

## Second build: shaped hulls and the engine line

Owner, 2026-10-17: "the real answer is you need to also make new thruster engines, maybe some go from 0-100% faster but have less max thrust so theres a give and take, but also it makes sense for thrusters meant to rotate rather than be the main thrust, and maybe larger engines too, for the bigger ships. also in your building of the ships you missed to use a lot of the hull block options that could honestly make some of these ships look less blocky or more sleek where it matters."

![The racers in game, second build](img/racers_in_game_second_build.png)

**Hull shaping.** The first build only cut the dome tops, so every outline was a cube staircase. The exporter now does four things:
- shapes each hull as a height field: domes rise from the outline and are chamfered underneath, while plates, cabins and barrels stand proud with chamfered edges;
- gives every cut cell its real cut planes, outline walls included;
- snaps outline edges within 8.5° of a kit line (along the grid, 45° or 1:2) onto that line through cell corners, so wedges and long slopes join into straight edges;
- runs slopes only along the grid, because a slope seated across a diagonal left a ragged patchwork.

The Gimbal's ring is an octagon, because a circle has no grid-friendly edge. The builder now seats 140–610 long slopes and 190–680 wedges or hip corners per ship.

**Engines** ([ADR-0120](../../decisions/ADR-0120-engine-line.md)):

| Ship | Mass | Engines | 0.5 s | 1 s | 2 s | 4 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Needle | 33 t | 2 sprint M, sprint S, 4 verniers on the pods | 12.6 | 23.8 | 48.0 | 96.3 m/s |
| Kite | 51 t | 3 sprint M, 4 wingtip verniers | 9.2 | 17.6 | 35.4 | 71.1 |
| Gimbal | 25 t | 8 ring sprint S pointing out of the ring, tail sprint S | 10.6 | 19.9 | 39.8 | 79.6 |
| Battering Ram | 140 t | 3 torch L (one on the bolted port pod), sprint M, 4 verniers | 3.7 | 10.0 | 26.6 | 60.5 |
| Hammerhead | 86 t | 2 torch L inboard, 2 sprint M outboard, 4 verniers on the hammer tips | 7.0 | 15.0 | 37.4 | 83.2 |

The Rams launch slowly while their torches spool, then pull harder than any Dart. They keep pushing for about half a second after release (peak speed at 4.5 s on a 4 s burn). The Gimbal was measured before its ring became an octagon.

## First build (RACE-008, RACE-013)

All six are built from the approved plans: `ArtSource/Racing/export_racers.py` extrudes them into 0.5 m voxel cells, and `RacerShipBuilder` places blocks with the NOCTURNE builder's kit matching. Each one spawns in the playground, passes ship-editor validation and flies.
