# Racer ships: silhouette options (RACE-003)

Two racers, three silhouette options each, for owner approval before any voxel build (SPEC-92 decision 2). The sheet is generated by `ArtSource/Racing/racer_silhouettes.py`.

![Racer silhouettes](img/racer_silhouettes.png)

| | Dart (Vestari) | Ram (Khuchad Clan) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Light, fast, avoids contact | Heavy, slow, makes contact |
| Hit force | Every hit throws it across the track | Barely moves; its impact driver shoves rivals and rocks |
| Ability | SLIP: a sideways dash, 6 s cooldown | SHOVE: a 60 m radial repulsor pulse, no damage, 10 s cooldown |
| Counter | The Ram, repulsor mines, engine wash | A clean Dart, EMP mines, tight hairpins |

Options:

| | Dart | Ram |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Needle.** A spear courier on one accelerator spine, with engine pods on swept pylons | **Battering ram.** A spiked wedge prow with the driver barrel through it, plus a salvaged pod bolted to port |
| 2 | **Kite.** A flying wing with a heat sail of radiator fins, so it can boost longer | **Salvage tug.** A grapple crane that tethers to pylons, wrecks and rivals, and a bumper bow |
| 3 | **Gimbal.** A cockpit pod inside a ring of eight thrusters; it corners best and is a circle silhouette | **Hammerhead.** A dock-bumper hammer with an impact driver at each tip, to spin rivals |

My picks, if you want a default:
- **Needle against Battering Ram:** the clearest contrast in shape and in role.
- **Gimbal and the Salvage tug** are the most novel, because each brings a mechanic: omni-thrust, and the tether.

The ratings are design intent. RACE-008 builds the approved shapes from DESIGN-GUIDE blocks and measures them.
